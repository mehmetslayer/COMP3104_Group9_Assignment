George Brown College (GBC) is a public college in downtown Toronto, Ontario, founded in 1967 and named after the Scottish-born journalist and Father of Confederation George Brown.

GBC has three main campuses: St. James (downtown core), Casa Loma (home of the technology and construction programs) and Waterfront (health sciences). About 30,000 full-time students study across more than 170 programs.

The School of Computer Technology at Casa Loma offers the Computer Programming and Analysis advanced diploma, which includes courses such as DevOps, full stack development, mobile development and a two-semester capstone project with industry partners.

What makes GBC different is its focus on applied learning: field placements, co-op terms and project-based courses mean students graduate with real work experience, not only theory.

The software development lifecycle (SDLC) describes the stages software goes through: planning, requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment and maintenance. Traditionally these stages were handled by separate teams, and code was handed over to operations only at the end.

DevOps changes this by connecting the stages into one continuous loop. Code is integrated and tested automatically on every commit (continuous integration), and releases are built and deployed through a pipeline instead of by hand (continuous delivery). Problems are found within minutes of a change rather than weeks later.

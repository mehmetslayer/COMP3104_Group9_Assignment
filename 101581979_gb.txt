git George Brown College

George Brown College is located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
It offers career-focused programs in areas such as technology, business,
health sciences, design, hospitality, and community services.

George Brown College has multiple campuses in downtown Toronto.
The college focuses on practical learning and prepares students for careers
through hands-on projects, labs, and industry-related education.

George Brown College provides student support services, academic resources,
career services, and learning opportunities to help students succeed during
their studies and prepare for employment.

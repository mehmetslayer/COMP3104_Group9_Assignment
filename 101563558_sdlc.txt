Why is DevOps important for the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)?

DevOps is the collaboration between the Development and Operations team, that
makes software launching faster and stable.

Previously, these teams would work in an isolated way, which lead to major issues 
like delays, frequent bugs and miscommunication. Such consequences could hinder
the quality of the product and slow down production.

Therefore, with the creation of DevOps, teams are able to improve the SDLC by
implementing better practices and principles, and collaborate to meet a common goal.

George Brown Polytechnic Info

George Brown Polytechnic, formerly George Brown College, is a public college located in downtown Toronto.
It opened in 1967 and took on its new name in 2025. The school is named after George Brown,
a politician and newspaper publisher who was one of the Fathers of Confederation.

It has three campuses (St. James, Casa Loma and Waterfront) and offers over 170 full-time programs in areas
like business, health sciences, engineering technologies and hospitality. Its programs focus on practical
skills and job readiness, with industry partners helping shape the curriculum.
